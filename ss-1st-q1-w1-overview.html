📜 rules

Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

Do you notice that our virtual classroom has certain rules, like raising my hand or muting my microphone? I wonder what would happen if our classroom didn’t have any rules — and why some rules are the same at school, at home, and online. I wonder how my responsibilities help my whole class, not just me.
