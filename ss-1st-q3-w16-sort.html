Choices Workshop Sort

The shoemaker, the elves, and the Velveteen Rabbit all have to make choices. Sort the pictures to find out why, and what each choice costs.

Tap a picture, then tap the bin where it belongs.

You did it! Now you know two big words.

ScarcityThere is not enough of something for everyone to have all they want. So people must make choices.
Opportunity costWhat you give up when you choose one thing instead of another.
