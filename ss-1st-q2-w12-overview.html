Key Terms
Vocabulary Words

Good

Something you can touch, hold, or see — like an apple, a jacket, or a toy.
🤝
Service
Tap to reveal definition
A job someone does to help others — like a farmer picking crops or a baker making pies.

Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

What do you notice about the difference between the apples you can hold and the baker who makes apple pies? What do you wonder about how many different workers it takes to get one apple from a tree to your kitchen?
